Decision Rule
If Mid Cheshire goes sub-18 and Colshaw sub-39, sub-2:55 is on. If either misses by >30s, MP target drops to 6:45/mi. Train for 2:55, race for 2:57.
DURING THE BUILD
MAINTAIN
Don't lose strength while running high mileage
Why This Matters
Skinny + weak = injury equation. Quad-dominant running, weak core, poor late-marathon form. Most sub-3 runners have functional strength even though they look skinny. Goal: build durability, not mass.
Benchmarks
START · MAINTAIN · BUILD
Phase Plan
May–Sep · Marathon Build
MAINTAIN PHASE
Upper Mon, Lower Wed, Core Fri. Moderate loads (8–12 reps). Build core specifically — plank progressions, side planks, dead bugs. No hypertrophy push.
Oct 2026 onwards · Hypertrophy Block
BUILD PHASE · POST-WARSAW
After A-race recovery (2 weeks easy). Caloric surplus +250–400 kcal. Lift 3–4×/week, heavy (4–8 reps). Reduce running to 30–40mi base. 4–6 month block to genuinely add muscle. Spring 2027 marathon would benefit from this strength.
Non-Negotiables
Core 3×/week — even on tired weeks. Plank + side plank + dead bug + Pallof press. 10 minutes.
Posterior chain weekly — RDLs or single-leg RDLs. Fix the quad-dominant pattern.
Don't skip Upper — atrophy at high mileage is real. Maintain bench, row, pull.
Yoga + Group Classes
AD-HOC SUPPLEMENTS
Yoga · 1×/week ideal
Hip openers (pigeon, lizard, half-pigeon) directly address ITB / knee tightness. Yin or restorative > vinyasa — slower holds, no extra load. Log via Quick Log Yoga button on Gym tab.
Group strength classes
Pick carefully. Runner-focused or coach-led classes that respect your training load = good. Generic CrossFit/HIIT/F45 = bad — they push to failure and run their own progression that fights your marathon plan. Log via Quick Log Class.

Core Plan
10 MIN · RUNNER-SPECIFIC
BLOCK A · ANTI-EXTENSION / ANTI-ROTATION
A1 Front plank45 SEC · TARGET 60+
A2 Side plank L30 SEC · TARGET 45+
A3 Side plank R30 SEC · TARGET 45+
A4 Dead bug8 EACH SIDE · SLOW
BLOCK B · POSTERIOR CHAIN ACTIVATION
B1 Glute bridge12 REPS · 2-SEC PAUSE TOP
B2 Bird dog8 EACH · SLOW + PAUSE
B3 Pallof press hold20 SEC EACH · SKIP IF NO BAND
B4 Single-leg glute bridge6 REPS EACH SIDE
FORMAT
Circuit: 2 rounds, minimal rest within round, 60–90 sec between rounds.
When: morning, pre-food, pre-run is best.
Activation mode: 1 round only — use the day before quality sessions.
FORM PRIORITIES
Plank: no sag, no pike. Straight line ear-to-heel.
Glute bridge: driven by glute squeeze, NOT hamstrings. If you feel it in hamstrings, knees too far away.
Dead bug: minimum 4 seconds per rep. Lower back stays glued to floor — if it lifts, smaller range.
Bird dog: hips don't tilt. Imagine a glass of water on your back.
IF TIME-PRESSED
The four that matter most: front plank, side planks both sides, dead bug, single-leg glute bridge. 5 minutes covers it.
PROGRESSION · EVERY 4 WEEKS
Increase hold times. Add unilateral variants. Add load (weighted plank, banded glute bridge). Track weekly via the Progress chart on the gym tab.
